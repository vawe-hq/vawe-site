A page has layers, like any film

Each colour means one kind of layer, the same in the studio, the components and the templates. It always sits beside its name.

TextWords that type, decode, slam or fill the frame.
MediaImages, clips and screenshots of your product.
ShapeSVG and CSS shapes that move on a spring.
GroupLayers that move as one.
CaptionWords timed to the sound.
SoundAudio tags: small effects on actions.
CameraPush, pan and depth of field on the whole frame.
EffectBloom and other passes on the whole image.
